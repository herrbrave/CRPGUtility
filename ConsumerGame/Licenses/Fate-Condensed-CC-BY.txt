(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Required attribution, verbatim (fetched directly from
https://fate-srd.com/official-licensing-fate/cc, not paraphrased):

"This work is based on Fate Condensed (found at https://www.faterpg.com/), a

Attribution 3.0 Unported license."

Scope of what was adapted (for clarity, not part of the required notice):
- The core 4dF ("Fudge dice") resolution mechanic and the Fail/Tie/Succeed/
  Succeed-with-Style outcome concept (the last three collapsed to a simple
  binary outcome in this project's dialogue checks -- see
  Docs/Verification/Consumer-Game.md for the stated simplifications).
- The Stress/Taken-Out damage model (modeled here as a single unified
  Stress track, not Fate's real separate Physical/Mental tracks, and
  without Consequences).
- Fate Points as a resource spent to invoke a bonus.
- The Stunt concept (a situational flat bonus).

This is a deliberately partial, "compact" implementation for a small test
game, not a claim of full Fate Condensed rules compliance. No Evil Hat
Productions text, art, or other copyrighted material is reproduced here
beyond the required attribution line above.

Choosing CC-BY over the OGL 1.0a route (also offered by Evil Hat for Fate
Condensed) was deliberate: CC-BY only requires this attribution block, per
Evil Hat's own description of it as "the easiest option to implement and
understand" for a simple derivative work with no lengthy Open Game
Content/Product Identity declarations to draft.
